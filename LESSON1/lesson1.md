LESSON 1.4: WHY DO WE NEED PROGRAMMING?

Why Human Language Is Problematic

Human language is filled with ambiguity. Consider these examples:

    "Please send the file." — Via email? WhatsApp? Shared folder? Which file?
    "Add salt to taste." — What does "to taste" mean to a computer?
    "Arrange the chairs." — In what pattern? How many? Where?
    "Work until it's done." — When is it done? How do I know?
Computers need specificity. They need to know:

    What exactly to do
    With what data or resources
    When to start and stop
    How to handle variations or errors
    What the expected result looks like
